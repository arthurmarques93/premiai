How to join an event

Tap the invite link, or open the app and choose Join. Enter the event code and your name. No password needed: your participation is linked to your device. If the host requires an account, sign in with Google, Apple or a username and password.

How to host

Choose Host, create your account and name your event. It comes with suggested categories you can change. Send the invite, open nominations and then the final vote among the top nominees. Results show up live.

Premium

Each account's first event comes with everything unlocked. After that, the free version has voting with up to 6 categories and shows a few ads. Premium is a one-time purchase that removes ads and unlocks RSVPs, check-in, raffles, King and Queen, co-hosts and up to 30 categories in every event you create. New phone? Tap Restore purchase on the Premium screen, signed in with the same account.

Report content

In the app, touch and hold a nomination or a person and choose Report. You can also email premiaiapp@gmail.com with the event code. We review reports within 24 hours. See the terms of use.

Delete account

In the app: in the Account tab, tap Delete account.

Without the app: email premiaiapp@gmail.com with the subject Delete account, from the account's email address (or including its username). The deletion is done within 7 days and confirmed by email.

What is deleted: the account (name, email, username, profile photo), its votes, nominations and RSVPs, and the events it organizes alone. Nothing is kept afterwards. Events with other organizers stay with them. See also the privacy policy.

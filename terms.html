What PremiAí is

PremiAí is an app for awards among friends: the host creates an event with categories, guests nominate and vote on their phones, and the results show up live. By using the app, you agree to these terms and to the privacy policy.

Zero tolerance for objectionable content

PremiAí does not tolerate objectionable content or abusive users. In nominations, names, photos, event names, categories or prizes, you may not post:

  • insults, hate speech or discrimination (race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, origin);
  • harassment, threats, bullying or exposing someone to humiliate them;
  • sexual content or nudity;
  • violence, illegal drugs or anything illegal;
  • other people's personal data without permission;
  • spam or scams.

Anyone who breaks these rules may have their content removed, be removed and blocked from the event, and have their account deleted.

How we moderate

  • Filter: texts with profanity or slurs are automatically rejected.
  • Report: touch and hold a nomination or a person and choose Report. The event host and the PremiAí team receive the report.
  • Host: can remove nominations and remove and block participants from the event.
  • PremiAí team: reviews reports within 24 hours and removes the content and whoever posted it when there is a violation.
  • Contact: premiaiapp@gmail.com.

Hosts

Whoever creates an event is responsible for inviting people, creating the categories and keeping an eye on what is posted. Categories should be good-natured fun that doesn't ridicule anyone.

Premium and ads

The free version shows some ads. Premium is a one-time purchase, made through the App Store, that unlocks extra features in the events you create and removes ads. Charges and refunds follow the store's rules (Apple).

Your account

You can delete your account at any time in the Account tab, or request it by email as explained in support.

Liability

Content posted in events is the responsibility of whoever posts it. PremiAí is provided as is and may change or go offline. We may update these terms; the current version is always on this page.
